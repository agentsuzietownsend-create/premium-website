ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Built around people.
Guided by purpose.

A people-focused partner for household support, recruitment and practical services across different work environments.

OUR STORY

More than a placement. A relationship built on clarity.

Haspol began as Haspol Jinim Resources, with a focus on connecting people to workforce opportunities and helping clients find staff for important everyday roles.

That experience informs the broader Haspol-Jinim Resources identity. Whether a request comes from a household or an organisation, our role is to understand the work involved and help clients begin the right staffing conversation.

We believe good service starts with realistic expectations, respectful communication and attention to the particular needs of each environment.
