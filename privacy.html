PRIVACY INFORMATION

Clear about your information.

How this website handles enquiries and the third-party services it links to.

Last reviewed: 14 November 2026. This page explains the functionality of the public website in its current release. It does not replace a business-specific privacy policy reviewed by the website owner and legal advisers.

Information entered into forms

The Request Staff page prepares a message using details you enter. This version processes those details in your browser. It does not store them in a Haspol website database or send them to a website server.

WhatsApp and email

When you use the WhatsApp button, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing your supplied details. You must confirm sending the message in WhatsApp. Messages you send are processed by WhatsApp and the receiving business account under their applicable policies. The email links open your own email application.

Candidate enquiries

This website does not collect CVs, identification documents or sensitive candidate information. Candidate enquiries are directed to the business contact channels. Do not send personal documents before checking the recruitment process directly with the business.

Photography and external resources

Illustrative photographs are served by Unsplash and are not represented as photographs of Haspol's actual team or clients. Fonts and other external resources may involve requests to their providers.

Contact

For privacy questions about information you personally send to the business, contact Abbexc@gmail.com.
