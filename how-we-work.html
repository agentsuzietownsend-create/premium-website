HOW WE WORK

Every great fit starts
with understanding.

From the first request to the next conversation, we help you share the information that matters.

OUR PROCESS

Four steps to a
clearer next step.

Different staffing assignments require different checks and arrangements. Our website helps organise the initial request while the Haspol team reviews the details with you.

01

Share your requirement

Tell us which role or service you need, the location, timeframe and work involved. The more useful the initial context, the easier it is to discuss appropriate next steps.

↗
02

Clarify the scope

The team considers the setting, duties, engagement type and any practical expectations. Additional details may be requested before an arrangement can be discussed.

↗
03

Discuss available options

Depending on the request, suitable staffing or support arrangements can be explored. Availability, screening steps and service-specific conditions must be confirmed.

↗
04

Agree how to proceed

Before any engagement, confirm responsibilities, service terms, fees if applicable and the arrangements for communication and follow-up.

↗
