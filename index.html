What kinds of workers can I ask about?

Published Haspol services include household personnel, drivers, office support, cleaning, security staffing, hospitality and industrial workers. Availability depends on the specific role and location.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Include the different roles or services in your enquiry so the team can review the combined scope.

Where does Haspol operate?

Historical business information mentions Lagos and Ogun State. Confirm the latest coverage and availability directly with the team.

Can I apply for employment?

Visit the Careers page to learn about candidate enquiries and how to ask about current openings.
